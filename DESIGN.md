# Design System: BSP Tech Solutions

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Logo-Blue Suite"**

The Amplemarket language, pinned by the user, built entirely from the BSP logo: navy ink #041D41 and logo blue #0057F7, plus the logo's own tagline grey and rule-line blue. The canvas is white with a cool blue wash. Headlines are large and light: weight 400 with negative tracking. Logo blue is the action color. Six business pillars are color-coded with tints and solids taken only from the logo's navy and blue, so the whole page reads as one brand with no extra hues. shadcn/ui on Base UI carries every control, re-themed through tokens.

The page alternates paper, wash and navy bands and closes on a navy-deep footer with a black-weight uppercase poster line whose second line glows in blue-on-dark. Content is assembled bento-style: photographs and pillar tiles sit side by side with 12px gutters inside a 1200px container, all with 12px corners. Depth is nearly absent: a 1px inset hairline on white cards, one soft multi-layer feature shadow under the hero image, the contact form and the floating WhatsApp button, and a header that frosts only once the page scrolls.

Confirmed rejections carried from the direction: walls of identical icon cards, and neon glow.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Two brand hues only: logo blue acts, navy inks; every tint derives from them.
- Weight-400 headlines with tracking that tightens as size grows.
- Six pillar tiles color-coded by role: four light blue/grey tints, two solids (blue, navy).
- Paper, wash and navy bands alternate; the footer is navy-deep with a two-tone poster headline.
- Short parallel points sit in ruled rows: a top hairline, a title, muted text, no fill.
- 8px buttons, 12px everything else.
- Nearly flat: hairline inset, one feature shadow, frost-on-scroll header.

## Colors

A two-hue palette drawn from the logo: navy and blue, with their tints and the tagline grey as the only neutrals.

### Primary
- **Logo Blue** (logo-blue): every primary action (filled buttons in hero, header, product panels, Cómo empezar, form submit, mobile menu), the active nav link, the dropdown icons and "Ver todos" row, the step numerals, the focus ring, text selection and caret. White on it clears 5.7:1. Hover deepens to **Logo Blue Strong** (logo-blue-strong, 6.5:1).

### Secondary
- **Ink Navy** (ink-navy): all text on light grounds, the integraciones, experiencia and contacto bands, and the infraestructura pillar.
- **Blue Mist** (blue-mist): shadcn `secondary` and `accent`; the segmented tab track, the icon chips and active row of the Servicios dropdown, and the icon chips in the mobile menu.

### Tertiary (pillar taxonomy)
Pillar colors are assigned by role, all derived from navy and blue:
- **POS tint** (pillar-pos): CuadraPOS. Navy text.
- **ERP tint** (pillar-erp): CuadraERP, a navy-grey. Navy text.
- **WMS tint** (pillar-wms): WMS con RFID, the deepest light tint. Navy text.
- **Integraciones solid** (pillar-int): logo blue. White text.
- **Facturación electrónica tint** (pillar-fe): the palest tint; it carries the hairline inset so it does not dissolve into paper. Navy text.
- **Infraestructura solid** (pillar-infra): navy. White text; also the infrastructure list panel.

### Neutral
- **Paper** (paper): page ground, white cards, inputs, text on solids and navy.
- **Wash** (wash): alternate section ground (productos, desarrollo, preguntas) and the step cards; the hover fill for ghost, outline and inverse buttons and menu rows, and the open Servicios trigger (shadcn `muted`); the hero gradient settles into it.
- **Tagline Grey** (tagline-grey): ledes and secondary copy on paper, wash and blue mist (5.9 / 5.5 / 5.1:1). Taken from the logo's tagline.
- **Navy Deep** (navy-deep): footer ground.
- **Navy Input** (navy-input): reserved for fields on navy grounds.
- **Navy Muted** (navy-muted): ledes, labels, secondary copy on navy and the infra panel (9.1:1).
- **Rule Blue** (rule-blue): the logo's rule line; icons and arrow markers on navy, including the product-link arrows in the experiencia band (8.5:1).
- **Blue on Dark** (blue-on-dark): the second line of the footer poster only (5.1:1 on navy-deep).
- **Navy Border** (navy-border): hairline rules on navy and navy-deep; it opens the integration groups and the operating-context columns.
- **Hairline** (hairline) / **Input Stroke** (input-stroke): 10% and 14% ink borders on light grounds.
- **Destructive** (destructive): validation errors only.

**External brand color.** WhatsApp teal (#128C7E, hover #0E7468) appears only on the floating WhatsApp button and the form success icon, as the platform's own convention; the white glyph on it clears 4.1:1. Every other WhatsApp action is a logo-blue, outline or inverse button carrying the WhatsApp glyph in its label color. It is not part of the BSP palette and must not spread to other elements.

### Named Rules
**The Logo-Only Rule.** Every color on the page derives from the logo's navy (#041D41) and blue (#0057F7), their tints, the tagline grey or the rule-line blue. The single exception is WhatsApp green on the floating WhatsApp button and the form success icon.

**The Blue Acts Rule.** Logo blue fills every primary action and is the focus color. On navy-deep the action inverts to a white button with navy text.

**The Pillar Role Rule.** Each pillar keeps one color everywhere it appears: POS, ERP, WMS and facturación electrónica take light tints with navy text; integraciones is solid blue and infraestructura solid navy, both with white text. Never assign a pillar color decoratively.

**The Full-Strength-On-Solid Rule.** Text on the solid blue pillar is full white. Reduced-opacity copy is only for light grounds (navy at 75% on the product panel tints and the hero wash stays above 6:1) and for navy, where the secondary color is navy-muted.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Geist (via next/font, with ui-sans-serif, system-ui fallback)
**Body Font:** Geist
**Label/Mono Font:** Geist Mono is loaded for data and codes; the build does not use it yet, so no mono role is recorded.

**Character:** One grotesk, set light and tight at large sizes, so headlines read calm and precise. The only heavy moment is the footer poster.

### Hierarchy
- **Poster** (900, uppercase, 2.75rem / 4.25rem (sm) / 5.25rem (md), 0.85, -0.03em): the two-line footer closer; line two in blue-on-dark. Nowhere else.
- **Hero** (400, 4.25rem, 1, -0.05em): the hero headline at lg only; the footer poster borrows the size at sm.
- **Heading-lg** (400, 3.5rem, 1, -0.05em): hero headline from md (steps up to Hero at lg, centered) and most section headlines from md (left-aligned).
- **Heading / Heading-sm** (400, 2.75rem / 2.25rem, 1.1, -0.04em / -0.03em): mobile step of every headline; shorter section titles (pillars, beneficios, preguntas, contacto) at md.
- **Subheading** (400, 1.75rem, 1.1, -0.017em): product name inside the product panel.
- **Title** (400, 1.5rem, 1.2, -0.02em): pillar names, ruled-row titles, operating-context titles, service-group names, infra item names, step titles, form success heading.
- **Body-lg** (400, 1.25rem, 1.4, -0.02em): ledes under headlines.
- **Body** (400, 1rem; 0.95rem dense): lists, descriptions and dropdown labels; item titles in service lists go to 500.
- **Label** (400, 0.875rem): header nav links and CTA, dropdown descriptions and its "Ver todos" row, footer links and titles, integration group labels, form helper and legal lines.

### Named Rules
**The Light Headline Rule.** Every heading from title up is weight 400 with the negative tracking its token carries. Hierarchy comes from size, never from bold.

**The One Poster Rule.** The black-weight uppercase poster appears once per page, as the footer closer. Uppercase and weight 900 are used nowhere else.

## Layout

A centered 1200px container with 16px gutters (24px from sm). Sections stack 96px of vertical padding, 112-128px from md; each opens with a heading block (max ~48rem, 20px heading-to-lede) and places content 48px below.

The page reads as five groups. Inicio: the hero, then "Qué hacemos" (its heading beside a problem line in ink and a muted paragraph from xl, with a three-column ruled row below). Servicios: the six pillar tiles as the single service index, then productos, integraciones, infraestructura and desarrollo. Beneficios: the heading block sticky on lg (4 columns) beside four ruled rows in two columns. Experiencia: a navy band of three operating-context columns. Contacto: Cómo empezar, Preguntas, the contact band and the footer. Grounds run paper, wash (productos), navy (integraciones), paper, wash (desarrollo), paper, navy (experiencia), paper, wash (preguntas), navy (contacto), navy-deep.

Content is a 12px-gutter bento on a 12-column grid: photographs take 7 columns and their pillar tile or panel 5; the six pillar tiles run 3 across at lg, 2 at sm, 1 on mobile; service groups and steps are 3 equal cards from md. Ruled rows use a wider 32px column gap. Everything stacks below lg, image first.

The hero is centered (headline, lede, then the two CTAs side by side from sm, stacked on mobile) over a radial wash from top right (#3A7DFF through #D6E4FF and wash to white), with the full-container ecosystem image 64-80px below and the hero's bottom quarter masked to paper so the image straddles wash and white. The header is fixed and transparent at the top, frosting to 80% paper with a heavy blur and hairline inset after 8px of scroll; `scroll-padding-top: 5rem` keeps anchors clear. Nav moves into a right sheet below lg; the header CTA hides below sm. A 56px WhatsApp button floats bottom-right (16px inset, 24px from sm) on every viewport.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat, with a three-item vocabulary. Separation comes from ground changes (paper, wash, navy) and pillar color.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Hairline** (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px rgb(4 29 65 / 0.06)`): white cards on wash, the palest pillar tile, and the scrolled header. Used instead of a border.
- **Feature** (`box-shadow: 0 26px 60px -6px rgb(4 29 65 / 0.16), 0 28px 28px -14px rgb(4 29 65 / 0.03), 0 6px 6px -3px rgb(4 29 65 / 0.05), 0 1px 1px -0.5px rgb(4 29 65 / 0.05)`): the hero image, the contact form panel and the floating WhatsApp button.
- **Focus** (`3px ring of logo blue at 50%`): every focusable control; pillar tiles add a 2px offset.

### Named Rules
**The Earned Shadow Rule.** The feature shadow belongs only to the hero image, the conversion panel and the floating WhatsApp button. Tiles, media and inline buttons stay flat; white or near-white surfaces get the hairline inset, nothing more.

## Shapes

Two working corner sizes: 8px for buttons, tab triggers, menu rows and icon chips, 12px (`--radius` 0.75rem, `rounded-lg`) for inputs, selects, textareas, cards, pillar tiles, media frames, the dropdown panel and the form panel. The segmented tab track is 10px so its 8px triggers nest inside 4px padding. The hero image reveal clips at 12px from start to finish. The only circle is the floating WhatsApp button; no pills. Tiles and media carry no border; rules (hairline or navy-border) open ruled rows and columns and divide list items and footer rows.

## Components

### Buttons
Rectangular, light-weight labels, logo blue.
- **Shape:** 8px corners, 1px transparent border, label weight 400 at 1rem in `xl`.
- **Icons:** an action that opens WhatsApp carries a leading WhatsApp glyph; an in-page link carries a trailing arrow (arrow-down for "Conoce nuestras soluciones").
- **Primary:** logo-blue fill, white label; `xl` (48px) for the hero, product panels, Cómo empezar, form submit and mobile menu; `lg` (36px, weight 400, 0.875rem) in the header.
- **Hover / Focus:** hover deepens to logo-blue-strong; focus-visible shows a blue border and 3px 50% blue ring; pressing nudges down 1px.
- **Outline:** paper fill, 1px hairline border, navy label, wash on hover; the hero's "Conoce nuestras soluciones" (paper at 90% over the wash) and "Preguntar por WhatsApp" in Preguntas.
- **Inverse:** the outline action on the navy-deep footer with its border made transparent: white fill, navy label, wash on hover.
- **Ghost:** no fill, weight 400, wash fill on hover; header nav links and the Servicios trigger, which keeps the wash while its dropdown is open. The active link turns logo blue.

### CTA Button (single source)
Every call to action is an entry in one config with three actions: "demo" (primary, opens WhatsApp; labeled "Solicita una asesoría gratuita" in the hero, header and mobile menu and "Agenda una demo de …" in the product panels), "pregunta" (outline, "Preguntar por WhatsApp") and "soluciones" (outline link to #servicios). `<CTAButton>` renders it at `xl` by default; a placement may change size, label wording or the WhatsApp prefill, never the verb. WhatsApp actions open in a new tab with an aria-label that says so, and every anchor carries `data-cta-id` and `data-cta-location`. The mobile-menu CTA and the floating button resolve the same config.

**The One Source Rule.** A new CTA is a new entry or location in the config, rendered through `<CTAButton>`; no hand-built action links.

### Pillar Tiles (signature)
Six tiles at 12px corners, 20px padding, at least 192px tall from sm, 3 across at lg. They are the single service index, read from the same list as the nav dropdown and the footer's Servicios column. Title-size name on top; description and a 16px arrow at the bottom, both at full strength in the tile's text color. Hover lifts the tile 4px and slides the arrow 4px right over 300ms ease-out. No border (except the hairline inset on the facturación tint), no shadow, no top icon.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 12px.
- **Background:** pillar color for pillar-owned content (product panels in POS/ERP/WMS tints, the infra list in solid navy); paper on wash for service groups; paper on navy for the form.
- **Shadow Strategy:** hairline inset on white cards on wash; feature shadow on the form only.
- **Internal Padding:** 20-24px; 24px / 32px (sm) for panels.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** 44px tall, 12px corners, 1px input-stroke border, paper fill, 1rem text, 14px horizontal padding, labels above.
- **Focus:** blue border, 3px 50% blue ring, blue caret.
- **Error / Disabled:** destructive border with 20% destructive ring and message below; disabled at 50%.

The `xl` primary submit, "Enviar por WhatsApp" with a leading glyph, composes the message and opens WhatsApp; the helper line beneath the submit says so. Its success state replaces the form with a WhatsApp-green glyph, a title-size weight-400 heading and follow-up actions.

### Product Selector

Changing tabs fades the panel in and slides it up 8px over 500ms.

### Navigation
Fixed 64px header: official logo mark (28px) left; from lg a Base UI navigation menu ("Servicios" with a chevron, then Beneficios, Experiencia, Cómo empezar and Preguntas as ghost links); one blue `lg` CTA, "Solicita una asesoría gratuita" with the WhatsApp glyph, right. Rows enter staggered, fading and sliding 16px in from the right over 300ms, 40ms apart; no motion under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Navy Band
Full-bleed ink-navy section: weight-400 headline, navy-muted lede, rule-blue arrow markers, a borderless 12px-cornered photograph, and an index of columns opened by navy-border top rules. ## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** fill every primary action with logo blue and a white label, 8px corners, 48px tall for main CTAs; hover to logo-blue-strong.
- **Do** derive every new color from the logo's navy and blue, the tagline grey or the rule-line blue.
- **Do** set headings at weight 400 using the heading, subheading and title tokens with their built-in negative tracking.
- **Do** keep each pillar's role color: tints with navy text for POS, ERP, WMS and facturación electrónica; solid blue (integraciones) and solid navy (infraestructura) with white text.
- **Do** alternate paper, wash and ink-navy sections, and close the page on the navy-deep poster footer.
- **Do** compose photographs and tiles as a 12px-gutter bento with 12px corners.
- **Do** set short parallel points as ruled rows: a top rule, a title, muted text.
- **Do** add every call to action through the CTA config and `<CTAButton>`, with its location for tracking.
- **Do** use the hairline inset for white or near-white surfaces, and the feature shadow only for the hero image, the conversion panel and the floating WhatsApp button.

### Don't:
- **Don't** introduce hues outside the logo's navy and blue family; WhatsApp green stays on the floating button and the form success icon only.
- **Don't** bold headings; the poster is the only weight-900 and uppercase text.
- **Don't** reuse a pillar color for anything other than its pillar.
- **Don't** dim text on the solid blue pillar or on blue buttons; white stays full strength there.
- **Don't** build walls of identical cards with an icon on top, or add glow or neon effects.
- **Don't** add borders or shadows to tiles and media; corners and color separate them.
